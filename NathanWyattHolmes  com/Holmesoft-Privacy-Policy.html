Holmesoft Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Company: Holmesoft

Holmesoft respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, or use software applications developed and distributed by Holmesoft. Different applications have different functions, so the information handled depends on the features you choose to use.

1. Information Collected by Holmesoft

Holmesoft does not operate a general user-tracking or advertising infrastructure and does not sell users' personal information. Unless an application's documentation or an applicable feature notice says otherwise, Holmesoft does not independently collect personal information through the application.

2. Local File Processing — BPM Tagger

BPM Tagger analyzes MP3 files in a folder selected by the user, determines their beats per minute (BPM), and updates the BPM field in the files' metadata. This file analysis and metadata update are performed locally by the application. Holmesoft does not receive the MP3 files or their BPM metadata as part of this functionality.

Users remain responsible for the files they choose to analyze and modify.

3. AI-Enabled Features and Third-Party AI Providers

Some Holmesoft applications may offer features that connect to artificial intelligence models through third-party application programming interfaces (APIs). Supported integrations may include services provided by Google and OpenAI. The specific providers, models, and available integrations may vary by application and may change over time.

When you choose to use an AI-enabled feature, information needed to process your request may be transmitted to the AI provider selected or configured for that feature. Depending on the feature, this may include prompts, instructions, conversation context, or other content that you choose to submit or make available to the feature.

That information is processed by the third-party provider under its own privacy policy, terms, and data-handling practices. Holmesoft does not control how an independent AI provider retains or uses information it receives. Please review the provider's applicable policies before using its service, and do not submit confidential, sensitive, or personal information unless you are comfortable sharing it with that provider.

The AI provider and model available to you may depend on the application's configuration and the API credentials or service settings used. Holmesoft does not claim that every application supports every provider or model.

4. Information Sharing and Sale

Holmesoft does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information. Holmesoft does not share personal information for third-party advertising or marketing. Information that you submit to an AI-enabled feature may, however, be sent to the AI provider in order to perform the feature you requested, as described above.

5. Data Storage and Transmission

Data handled by an application may remain on your device or may be transmitted to a third-party service when required for a feature you choose to use. The handling of information depends on the application's functionality and configuration. Refer to the applicable application documentation for feature-specific details.

6. Third-Party Platforms and Services

Holmesoft software may be distributed through third-party platforms, including the Microsoft Store. Those platforms may independently collect information under their own privacy policies and terms. Holmesoft does not control the data practices of those platforms or of third-party services integrated into an application.

7. Children's Privacy

Holmesoft applications are not designed to collect personal information from children. Holmesoft does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its applications.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Holmesoft software and services evolve. The current version will be published at this web address. The effective date at the top of the page indicates when the policy was last revised.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Holmesoft's privacy practices, please contact Holmesoft through NathanWyattHolmes.com.
